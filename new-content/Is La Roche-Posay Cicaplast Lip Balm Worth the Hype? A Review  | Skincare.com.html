An Editor Reviews the La Roche-Posay Cicaplast Lip Balm

March 06, 2026
By: Ariel Wodarcyk | skincare.com by L'Oréal

Key Takeaways

  • A Targeted Formula, Not Just a Balm: This is a targeted formula for compromised lips. The formula, which includes 5% Panthenol (Vitamin B5), Shea Butter, and the brand's Thermal Spring Water, is designed to create a protective barrier that helps soothe the feeling of dryness.
  • Unique, Comfortable Texture: Unlike heavy, waxy balms, this has a lightweight cream texture formulated for fast absorption without a greasy or sticky residue. This makes it suitable for day-long wear and a good base as a smoothing primer under lipstick.
  • Dermatologist-Tested & Sensitive Skin-Friendly: Following the La Roche-Posay standard, the lip balm is dermatologist-tested, fragrance-free, and suitable for sensitive skin, making it a reliable choice for lips that are easily irritated.

Why It’s So Popular

The appeal of this lip balm is rooted in its straightforward, well-regarded ingredients, which are a hallmark of the La Roche-Posay brand. The star of the show is panthenol (vitamin B5), present at a concentration of 5%. Panthenol is a humectant known for its ability to attract and hold moisture, which is crucial for hydrating the lips. The formula also contains shea butter, a well-known emollient that helps soften and smooth the skin's surface. Finally, like all La Roche-Posay products, it's formulated with the brand's signature antioxidant-rich thermal spring water to help soothe the skin. It's also fragrance-free and paraben-free.

Ariel's Honest Review of La Roche-Posay Cicaplast Lips Hydration Restore Lip Balm

Even as a beauty editor and a self-described product fanatic, there are a lot of products I’ll slack on repurchasing — take, for example, the moisturizer I’ve been scraping out of a near-empty tub for the past few weeks, or the trickle of body wash left in my shower at this very moment (don’t worry, I’ve been subbing that with my dual face and body cleanser). Lip balm, however, is one thing I will never be caught without, especially as the weather cools down and the wind starts to pick up. I couldn’t wait to add the La Roche-Posay Cicaplast Lips Hydration Restore Lip Balm, which the brand kindly gifted me, to my collection. Below, learn more about the formula and find my full review.

This hydrating lip balm is housed in a small squeeze tube, which is perfect for sliding in my pocket or purse and reapplying while I'm out. It's fragrance-free, with a slightly creamy formula that sinks in almost instantly. The formula contains vitamin B5, also known as panthenol, shea butter, and thermal spring waters. The moisturizing formula helps keep my lips soft, smooth, flake-free, and perfectly primed for whatever lipstick I'm layering on top that day. I've passed around several tubes to share with friends, and all of us agree—this is a lip balm we'd reach for to help address dry, uncomfortable lips this winter.
